## Remaining observations (none blocking)

**1. A `verify_source` negative's `source.citation` is the passage's citation in its *true* source, under the *asked* edition's work.** Real example from the default run:

```
source:   {work: 'Sunan Abi Dawud', version: 'sunnah.com Arabic', citation: '17:79', requested_as: 'Abu Dawud', …}
expected: {answer: 'no', true_work: "The Qur'an", true_version: 'Uthmani (QUL)', true_language: 'ar'}
```

`'17:79'` is a Qur'an reference sitting in a record whose `work` is Sunan Abi Dawud, so `source.work + source.citation` reads as a hadith citation that does not exist. Nothing consumes it today (`score.py` doesn't; the viewer reads `expected.citation`), and the spec doesn't specify the field for negatives — so this isn't a contract violation. But it is committed evidence a human will audit, and `question_set_digest` binds it: changing it after Phase 6 means regenerating the run. Cheapest fix now: move it to `expected.true_citation` (which also makes a "no" ground truth auditable) and leave `source.citation` out for negatives, or document the semantics in the `Question` docstring. **Worth a decision before Phase 6, not after.**

**2. The containment guard passes by seed luck, not by construction.** It catches the deleted filter at `seed=1`, but across seeds 1–50 only 5 would — the fixture draws 4 negatives from 31 candidates of which 1 is the shared passage. Any future change to the fixture size, quota, or seed silently disarms it. The structural version (make *all but one* cross-family candidate contained in the asked edition) would fail 100% of the time with the filter gone. Cheap hardening; not required now.

**3. Negative candidates are pooled and shuffled uniformly over *entries*, so negative sources are weighted by corpus size, not uniformly over texts.** On the real corpus this is benign (each Arabic hadith's only same-language cross-family partner is the Qur'an anyway), but it's an implicit distributional choice — worth one line in the Phase-6 run README alongside the near-verbatim-shared-passage residual the architect already asked for.
