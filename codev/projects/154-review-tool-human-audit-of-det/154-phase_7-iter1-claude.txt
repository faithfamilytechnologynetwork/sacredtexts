3. **`PassageSource` gating is looser than its intent.** It renders for any cell whose `expected.true_citation` is a non-empty string rather than on `cell.task === "verify_source"`, and `parts` is computed before the early return. Harmless today (only `verify_source` expected dicts carry `true_*`), but the guard and the comment disagree about what selects it.
4. **The `/assets/*` fallback is the thing that made the blank page confusing.** The walk documented "restart after a rebuild" rather than changing behaviour, which is a defensible call — the fallback is inherited from the pinned viewer host and is phase-4 scope. But a missing bundle returning the SPA shell with a 200 is exactly the silent fallback the project's fail-fast principle warns about. Excluding `/assets/*` from `notFoundHandler` the way `/api/*` already is would turn a blank page into a legible 404. Worth considering, not here.
5. The new "Passage source" line was verified in jsdom, not in the browser pass that preceded it. Very low risk (plain text in an already-exercised layout), but the walk step wasn't re-run after the fix.
