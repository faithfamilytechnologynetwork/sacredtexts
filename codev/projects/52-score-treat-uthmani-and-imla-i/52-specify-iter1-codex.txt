KEY_ISSUES:
- The proposed letter folds do not equate `يَـٰبُنَىَّ` with Imla'i `يَا بُنَيَّ`: after dagger-alif and ى folding, one retains `يابني` and the other `يا بني`. The experiment’s quick fold removed whitespace, but the specification neither requires nor bounds such a rule. Define the intended Arabic word-boundary equivalence and add positive and negative tests; globally deleting Arabic whitespace could substantially change containment and token-F1 behavior.
- The #48 coordination constraint says orthography flips have `correct` changing 0→1 while strict `normalized` remains 0. That contradicts changing `normalize()` itself: direct orthography-only matches should make `normalized` change to 1, with possible `token_f1` changes too. Define the pre-registered delta using before/after projections or explicit cell sets rather than the containment-rebaseline signal.
- Several equivalence rules are insufficiently specified. “Standalone hamza” has no stated counterpart or canonical result, and dropping it does not solve the cited hamza-seating pair; that pair requires ء to behave like ا in context. The `ىٰ → ا` digraph must also take precedence over the general `ى → ي` rule. State the required equivalence examples/results clearly enough to avoid incompatible implementations.
- The Current State is factually inaccurate: `normalize()` already folds alif madda through NFKD, as pinned by `test_arabic_folds_alef_variants_and_tatweel`. Also, the existing U+0590–U+08FF mark range affects Syriac, Thaana, N’Ko, Samaritan, and Mandaic marks, so it does not leave “every other script” unchanged. Correct these claims and require explicit Hebrew/non-Arabic regression coverage for the new fold.
- The collision gate needs a testable acceptance rule and failure disposition. Full-unit equality alone is weaker than the actual containment scorer; require zero new collisions/containment relationships at the scoring-predicate granularity, and specify that discovered collisions block the proposed rule pending review.
- Rebaseline scope remains “at minimum” despite being called confirmed. Require discovery of all committed affected runs and regeneration of every dependent artifact, including the original run’s `summary` and FAccT card, while retaining frozen pre-change witnesses rather than ambiguously “re-pinning” away the evidence.
