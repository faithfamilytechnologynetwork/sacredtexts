### On the design

Putting the optionality in the *pattern* (`ا?` per reference dagger) rather than in a canonical string is correct and the reasoning in `score.py:158-189` is the clearest statement of why #57's `normalize()`-side materialisation was wrong (it un-credits the defective class هذا/ذلك/الرحمن). Keeping `normalize()` byte-stable means the generation `ANCHOR_DIGEST` and the copyright-audit path are untouched — I confirmed the audit runs inside the green suite.

The disclosed residuals are the mark of an honest rebaseline: the 8-cell `ءَا↔آ` under-credit is pinned by identity as #60's ready-made oracle, and the tutorial README states outright that ~2–3 pts of the headline haiku→haiku-search "search lift" is that residual rather than retrieval. Self-correcting a "1 cell" claim into "8 cells with an asymmetry" is the kind of thing that usually doesn't survive into a PR body.

### Minor items (none blocking)

1. **Stale module docstring** — `outcomes.py:47` still lists `bounded_contains` among "the primitives … imported lazily"; the import is now `fold_aware_contains`. One-word fix, can ride a later commit.
2. **`_single_citation_correct` inherits the verse-length fold.** Verified a genuine no-op today (I counted **0** Arabic-script citations across all committed evidence), and `ARABIC_CELLS` does count `expected.citation`, so a future Arabic citation trips the pin. But the moment #39 introduces one, the citation verdict silently becomes looser (whitespace-flex + optional alef on a short string is where the 10-bucket collision residue actually bites). The inline NOTE says "revisit then" — given the project's fail-fast principle, a hard guard (or simply leaving citations on `bounded_contains`) would be the more conservative shape.
3. **Scope of "only ADDS credit."** Under the default graders it is exactly true. Under `--evaluator strict`, `_with_outcome`'s `classify_refusal(...) and not correct` means a decline-prefixed Imla'i delivery now lands `incorrect` instead of `refused` — coverage ↑, accuracy ↓. That is defensible (it *is* a delivery, and it's the same rule `bounded_contains` already applied), the review already scopes the claim to "under the default graders," and no committed run uses strict. Worth one line in the `fold_aware_contains` docstring so a future reader doesn't over-read the superset guarantee.
4. **Benchmark signal, for the working group rather than for the code:** haiku-search is now 33/33 on the Qur'an cut (100% on the Sunni Islam slice) and haiku 30/33. The Arabic subset is at ceiling for these arms, which is worth knowing when designing #39's difficulty.
