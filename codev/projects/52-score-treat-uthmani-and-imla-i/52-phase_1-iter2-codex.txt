KEY_ISSUES:
- `arabic_ws_insensitive_contains` treats Arabic-Indic digits as Arabic and removes every normalized space before inserting `\s*` (`score.py:245-268`). Consequently `١ ٣` matches `١٣`, and `الآية ١:٣` matches `الآية ١٣`, recreating the exact bounded-containment failure the iteration addressed. Whitespace flexibility should be limited to Arabic-letter boundaries while preserving numeric separators; add Arabic-Indic digit negatives.
- `_ACCEPTED_DISTINCT_WORD_MERGES` freezes only normalized bucket keys, not their members (`test_arabic_orthography_fold.py:162-220`). A newly merged word entering an existing accepted bucket leaves `distinct_word_merges` unchanged and passes, contrary to the spec’s frozen-residue guarantee. Pin each accepted bucket’s exact corpus members or strict skeleton/member sets.
